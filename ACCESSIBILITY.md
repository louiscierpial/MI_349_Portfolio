**Initial Accessibility Audit**



Performed on November 3rd, 2026 at 3:45pm using https://louiscierpial.netlify.app/.

Accessibility score was a 93/100 on both mobile and desktop. The only issue was: "Background and foreground colors do not have a sufficient contrast ratio."

More specifically, the light purple background of the footer was too light (#706993), making the readability of the white text inside of it poor.



To fix this, I chose a new color (#0A2463) to replace this background color, keeping the text as it was. After making the fix, my website received a 100/100 score for accessibility on both mobile and desktop.
